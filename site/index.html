Reverse Reparations

After Antony Anghie, “The Injustices of Reparations,” American Journal of International Law 119:3 (2025)

Claims for colonial reparations are told they come too late, or that the law at the time allowed what was done. Meanwhile money has long flowed the other way: from the colonized to the colonizers, and now from poorer states to foreign investors and creditors. This page lays out the cases Anghie points to, with sources for every figure.
